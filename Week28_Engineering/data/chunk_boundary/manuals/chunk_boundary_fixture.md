## Code and explanation
Call `send_logs()` to export diagnostic logs. Save the returned archive locally before attaching it to a support ticket.

## Multi-fact boundary
For firmware 2.1, hold Power for 10 seconds before pressing Recovery. For firmware 2.2, hold Power for 5 seconds and wait for the blue indicator.

## Long section
The recovery procedure must be completed in order. First back up local notes and connect the supplied charger. Keep the device powered during the update. Do not disconnect the cable while the status indicator is pulsing. When the update completes, wait for the restart before opening the service screen. If the screen remains blank after restart, reconnect the display cable and restart once. These details belong to one long section and should not be lost at chunk boundaries.

## Overlap rescue
A distinctive diagnostic token occurs at the end of one paragraph and the supporting explanation begins in the next: code AX-17-ZEBRA-731 means the storage cache is full; move archived logs to an external drive before retrying.
